• 58 metals and mining companies above ₹500 crore make their decisions from Maharashtra: 38 listed (11 of them adjacent: auto castings, explosives, abrasives, electrodes) and 20 unlisted. Four hubs: Mumbai for the conglomerates (Tata Steel, JSW, Hindalco, Welspun, Lloyds, Mukand) and the copper wire makers; Pune for forgings and castings (Kalyani, Kirloskar, CIE, Alicon); Nagpur and Vidarbha for mining and steel (MOIL, Western Coalfields, Sunflag, NECO, Solar, Caliber); and Jalna, a family-owned secondary steel cluster. verified
  • Unlike Kolkata, the listed giants dominate. Listed companies report about ₹8.5 lakh crore of consolidated revenue (including Novelis and Tata Steel Europe), against about ₹1.1 lakh crore for unlisted companies. Much of the unlisted revenue sits in subsidiaries of listed groups (JSW Steel Coated Products alone is about ₹34,500 crore) or multinational units. Independent unlisted family businesses are concentrated in Jalna. verified
  • Decided in Mumbai, built elsewhere. Of 146 plant locations, 61 are in Maharashtra. The rest are spread across Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Jharkhand. A Mumbai relationship therefore carries work in many states, for example JSW in Karnataka, Hindalco in Odisha and Madhya Pradesh, and Tata Steel in Jharkhand and Odisha. interpretation
  • Family links are far more visible here, because the large groups' promoter filings list married daughters.
    • O.P. Jindal's daughters appear as Bhartia, Goel, Bhuwalka and Jhunjhunwala, and Sajjan Jindal's daughters as Handa and Shete. Sangita Jindal was born a Kanoria, from Kolkata. Naveen Jindal's daughter married into the Somany family in December 2025.
    • Noel Tata is married to Aloo Mistry, which ties the Tata Trusts to the Shapoorji Pallonji group. Neerja Birla was born a Kasliwal, of S. Kumars.
    • Endurance's Anurang Jain is Rahul Bajaj's nephew, and his twin brother Tarang runs Varroc. The Kabras of Ram Ratna Wires and RR Kabel are linked to the Loya family by marriage.

  • Splits and disputes decide whom to approach.
    • Kirloskar: Sanjay against his younger brothers Atul and Rahul. Kirloskar Industries still holds about 24% of Sanjay's Kirloskar Brothers.
    • Kalyani: Baba Kalyani against his sister Sugandha Hiremath, in Supreme Court mediation since July 2026.
    • Mansukhani brothers: split in 2013.
    • Kabra family: split into Ram Ratna Wires and RR Kabel, which still hold shares in each other.
    • Jindal family: four separate branches.

  • Plants that have changed owners bring incentive questions.
    • Evonith at Wardha went from the Lloyds group to the Miglani family's Uttam group in 2012, then to Nithia Capital and CarVal through insolvency in 2021.
    • JSW took Ispat's Dolvi plant in 2010.
    • JSW and JFE took over thyssenkrupp's Nashik electrical steel unit in 2025.
    New owners usually need to re-establish whether unexpired state incentives carry over after a change of ownership. That makes it a natural opening for the incentive practice. interpretation
