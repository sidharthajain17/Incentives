What the Government Resolution actually changes

Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 · Analysis against the Resolution of 8 September 2026 · Prepared 13 November 2026

In short

The Resolution has now been read in full, and it differs from the published commentary in ways that matter commercially. The slab tables were being reported correctly. Almost everything around them was not.

Three findings change advice. Every established industrial address in Gujarat sits on the lower band, with only Dholera and Becharaji on the higher one. Jobs, not capital, buy the higher tiers — a project above ₹1,000 crore that misses the employment test falls back rather than qualifying. And electricity drawn from a captive plant earns no power tariff subsidy at all, while renewable power through open access earns the full rate.

One structural point sits above all of them. Gujarat's own sector-specific schemes are mutually exclusive with this one, while central schemes stack up to the value of eligible investment. Choosing between the general policy and a state sectoral policy is therefore a real decision with a real cost, not an exercise in collecting both.

And three deadlines are live now, the first of them roughly three weeks away.

The seven findings

1Every flagship industrial address is on the lower band

The Resolution classifies 268 talukas into Category A, which carries the higher entitlement, and Category B, which carries the lower. A taluka is the sub-district administrative unit. Category A is the less industrially developed set — and the consequence is that Gujarat's best-known industrial destinations are almost all on the lower band.

Category B — lower bandDahej (Vagra) · Ankleshwar · Jhagadia · Sanand · Mandal · Hazira (Chorasi) · Halol · Vapi · Savli · Padra · Mundra · Anjar · Gandhidham · Morbi · Wankaner · Ahmedabad · Surat · Vadodara · Rajkot · Bhuj
Category A — higher bandDholera · Becharaji · and otherwise the tribal belt (Netrang, Dediapada, Sagbara, Umarpada, Kaprada, Dharampur), inland Kachchh (Rapar, Nakhatrana, Lakhpat, Mandvi), Saurashtra interior and the Dangs

The gap is 5 to 10 percentage points of eligible fixed capital investment with no change whatever to the project, and the power tariff rate doubles from ₹1 to ₹2 per unit on the same split. The state has deliberately priced its established corridor below its undeveloped talukas, because the corridor does not need a subsidy to attract investment. interpretation

The Mandal-Becharaji point deserves isolating. Mandal taluka sits in Ahmedabad district and is Category B. Becharaji taluka sits in Mehsana district and is Category A. The Mandal-Becharaji Special Investment Region straddles both. Plot selection within a single investment region moves the band.

2Jobs buy the higher tiers, not capital

The Resolution deals expressly with a large project that misses the employment test, and the treatment is a fall-back rather than a qualification.

 Mega and Ultra Mega status is confined to thrust sectors. A ₹15,000 crore general-sector project is held to 20% of eligible investment however large it becomes. The employment escalator also compounds: a ₹20,000 crore Ultra Mega project carries 4,000 direct jobs, not 3,000. fact

For a capital-intensive, low-headcount project — which describes a good deal of Gujarat's chemical and metals base — this is the provision that decides the outcome, and it should be modelled at feasibility stage rather than discovered at application.

3The captive power trade-off

Three provisions interact, and read together they create a genuine choice on any electricity-intensive project.

ProvisionEffect
Capital cost of a captive power plant within the premisesCounts as eligible investment
Independent power plant, including renewableExcluded from eligible investment
Electricity drawn from a distribution company, or renewable power through open accessEarns the power tariff subsidy
Electricity consumed from the unit's own captive plantEarns no power tariff subsidy
 fact For an expansion, the power tariff subsidy applies only to additional consumption, and a sub-meter must be installed.

So the choice is between a one-off capital allowance and a recurring consumption subsidy. Build captive, and the plant cost enters the subsidy base but every unit consumed earns nothing. Draw from the grid or through renewable open access, and every unit earns ₹2 in Category A or ₹1 in Category B, up to 20–25% of eligible investment depending on tier.

For a high-consumption project the second route can be worth materially more, and the renewable open access limb means it need not mean buying expensive grid power. This is worth a present-value comparison on any metals, chemicals or data-centre-adjacent load, and that comparison is a tool worth building once and reusing. interpretation

4The thrust sector list is open, not closed

Eligibility lists in state policies are usually exhaustive annexures, and most disputes turn on whether a product is on them. This one is drafted differently, in three respects that together make it argue-able.

  • The sub-sectors under each of the 16 thrust sectors are expressly indicative
  • Entry 17 is a residual clause — any other sector as decided from time to time by the committee chaired by the Industries Commissioner
  • Where a sub-sector is disputed, that committee decides by considering HSN code and the opinion of sector experts — the HSN code being the harmonised tariff classification already used for customs and GST

The practical consequence is that a product which looks marginal is an argument to be made rather than a door that is closed, and there is a named forum and a named test. fact

The listed sub-sectors are also broader than the commentary suggested. Worth noting for your sectors: cement, metals (ferrous and non-ferrous) and metal products, critical mineral processing, refractories and glass all sit inside Metals and Minerals; wires and cables and electrolysers sit inside Capital Equipment; electric vehicles and auto components inside Mobility; and there are full sub-sector lists for nuclear power equipment including small modular reactors and for semiconductor ancillary units.

One condition precedent to note: chemical and pharmaceutical units must have a captive effluent treatment plant or access to a common one.

5State schemes are mutually exclusive; central schemes stack

This is the structural finding, and it governs how the whole Gujarat incentive stack should be approached.

CombinationPosition
This scheme plus a central schemePermitted — capped so that total state and central incentives do not exceed eligible fixed capital investment
This scheme plus another Gujarat sector-specific schemeChoose one. Not eligible here if availing incentives for the same component under another state scheme

 fact

The stacking cap against central schemes is generous, and leaves real headroom above a 35% state entitlement. The choose-one rule against Gujarat's own sectoral policies is the constraining one. For a project that could sit under the electric vehicle, electronics, semiconductor, data centre or renewable energy policies, the general scheme and the sectoral scheme have to be valued against each other and one of them given up. That makes decoding the state sectoral layer the next piece of work, and makes it a genuine comparison rather than an addition.

6The effective rate decays above ₹35,700 crore

Each tier carries two annual caps running in parallel — a percentage of eligible investment and an absolute rupee figure — and the Resolution states in every tier that carry forward of incentive lost to the annual ceiling is not allowed. Entitlement above what can be drawn in a year is lost, not deferred.

Taking an Ultra Mega thrust project in Category A — 40% ceiling, 3.5% annual cap, ₹1,250 crore absolute cap, twelve years:

₹20,000 cr40.0%
₹35,700 cr40.0%
₹50,000 cr30.0%
₹75,000 cr20.0%
Eligible fixed capital investment, against the effective entitlement as a percentage of it.

The crossover sits at roughly ₹35,700 crore, being ₹1,250 crore divided by 3.5%. Below it the percentage cap governs and the headline holds. Above it the absolute cap governs, and every further rupee of investment earns a progressively lower effective rate. At ₹75,000 crore a 40% headline has become 20%.

For projects of that scale the customised package from the Chief Minister's High Power Committee is not an optimisation. It is the answer, and the arithmetic above is the argument to take into the room.

7Four provisions that quietly reduce the value
ProvisionWhat it does
Modernisation is outInvestment by an existing undertaking for renovation, modernisation, rehabilitation or rationalisation is expressly not eligible. Only new units, expansion and diversification qualify. A narrower project-character test than most state policies and easily assumed otherwise
The EPF testEPF reimbursement runs only for a "new employee", defined as someone who did not hold a Universal Account Number before joining. That excludes anyone previously in formal EPF-covered employment, so the reimbursement is worth far less on a plant staffed by experienced hires than the headline 100% suggests
Recovery on breachIncentives are recoverable as an arrear of land revenue, with interest at 18% a year running from the date of first availment — not from the breach. Undisbursed amounts are forfeited. Recovery as a land revenue arrear is a powerful mechanism, and the interest clock makes a breach found late considerably more expensive than the entitlement was worth
Short claim windowsThree months for the provisional certificate, three months for the stamp duty claim, three months for each of the selected-thrust support claims. The stamp duty window runs from production start, so it is easily missed where land was bought years earlier
 fact The continuing employment condition is 85% of total employees and 60% of managerial and supervisory staff domiciled in Gujarat, throughout the incentive period, with the 18% recovery behind it.

Where to go for value

Five levers the Resolution opens, in the order we would test them on a live project.

1 · Selected thrust at any scale

Sports goods, toys, footwear, robots and drones carry the 50% / 45% ceiling at every size class including micro, with no employment gate at all. On top of that sits a support package: 75% of intellectual property costs to ₹1 crore, 75% of technology acquisition to ₹5 crore, 100% of international certification fees to ₹5 crore, ₹15,000 per Gujarat-domiciled trainee per month for twelve months, and 50% of a creative design studio to ₹50 crore. This is the most generous position in the policy and the easiest to reach.

2 · Model grid or renewable open access against captive

On any power-intensive project, compare the one-off capital allowance on a captive plant against a recurring ₹2 or ₹1 per unit on every unit consumed for eight to twelve years. The renewable open access route qualifies for the subsidy, so the comparison is not simply subsidy against cheap power.

3 · Treat siting as an incentive decision

Where a project has genuine locational freedom, Dholera is the only major serviced destination on the higher band, and Becharaji the only other Category A taluka with an established industrial base. Where it does not, Category B is the price of being in the corridor and the argument moves to the customised package. Within Mandal-Becharaji, check the plot.

4 · Argue the product into a thrust sector

Indicative sub-sectors, a residual clause and HSN-based determination by a named committee together mean a marginal product is a case to be made. The difference between general and thrust is 20% against 35%.

5 · Take anything above ₹1,000 crore to the High Power Committee

The Chief Minister's committee can sanction customised packages for Mega and Ultra Mega projects in thrust and selected thrust sectors, and has express authority to decide any matter not expressly provided for in the Resolution, with its decision final and binding. It is also mandated to facilitate relocation of industries from other States. For a project of that size the published slabs are the floor.

One structuring question to resolve before it becomes a structure

The Resolution permits multiple projects at a single location to be treated as separate units for incentive purposes where separate books of account are kept and fixed capital investment is separately identifiable. It separately limits an undertaking to two availments during the policy period. Whether a genuine industrial complex of four projects draws four entitlements or two is not resolved on the face of the text. This is worth a clarification from the department before it is worth designing around, and commercial substance remains the guardrail in either case. interpretation
