Metals and mining: India market map

Prepared 23 Nov 2026 · Version 1 · 62 companies (23 Tier A, 39 Tier B) · 165 plant and mine site clusters across 20 states and union territories

Key takeaways

  • Decision offices and plants sit on two different maps. Of 23 Tier A companies, 17 have plants in the eastern mineral belt (Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal). Only 5 of them make decisions from there; most decide from Mumbai, Delhi NCR or Kolkata. Interpretation
  • Odisha has the most plant and mine sites (30). Steel, stainless, aluminium, ferro-alloys, coal, iron ore and mineral sands are all there, and it is also where most announced greenfield capacity is going. Maharashtra has a similar number of companies, but they lean towards downstream processing and head offices. Inferred
  • About ten groups (Tata, JSW, SAIL, AM/NS, Jindal, Hindalco, Vedanta, Coal India, NMDC, NALCO) account for most of the sector's plant sites. Each runs operations in several states, so each needs state-specific tax and incentive coverage under a single group relationship. Interpretation
  • Critical minerals and rare earths form a small but fast-moving segment. It is made up of public sector undertakings, a few mid-sized recyclers and diversifying majors (Hindustan Zinc, Hindalco, Coal India, GMDC). The National Critical Mineral Mission (Jan 2025) and the rare earth permanent magnet scheme (Nov 2025) are where new entrants are likely. Evolving
  • Tier B is under-counted by design in this version. There are many mid-sized secondary steel and ferro-alloy units in Raipur, Raigarh, Durgapur, Sambalpur and Barbil. A second pass can add them for the states you choose. Fact

Why this matters for coverage
  • The relationship is usually built at the decision office: CFO, group head of tax, head of strategy.
  • The work happens in the plant states: state GST registrations, state incentive claims and disbursement, royalty and mining-linked levies, and state-level disputes.
  • So a coverage model may be considered with a group lead at the decision office and a state lead in each plant state. It is especially worth evaluating for Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Karnataka, where many groups share the same state agencies.

Method and certainty

  • Tier test: Tier A means consolidated revenue above ₹10,000 crore. Tier B means ₹1,000 to 10,000 crore. Emerging critical-mineral players are shown as Tier B, with a note. Revenue is shown in bands, using the latest full year available.
  • Registered office is the legal address in company filings. Decision office is where leadership and the central finance and tax teams usually sit.
  • Certainty: Inferred: compiled from public company disclosures (annual reports, exchange filings) up to mid-2026. To be verified against FY26 annual reports. Rows tagged Evolving cover restructurings in progress: the Vedanta demerger, the Jindal Steel renaming, the Vizag Steel revival and the Thoothukudi copper closure.
