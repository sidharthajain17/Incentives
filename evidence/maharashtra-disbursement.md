# Maharashtra: evidence of incentive disbursement

Market evidence of how Maharashtra actually pays industrial incentives. Tagged as evidence of practice, not legal authority.

| Date of source | Unit | Location (zone) | What it shows | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 2025 (CARE rating rationale) | SRJ Peety Steels Pvt Ltd (secondary steel, billets and TMT) | Jalna, Marathwada (D+ zone under the Package Scheme of Incentives) | Has received the Industrial Promotion Subsidy since FY15: **₹278 crore received up to March 2025 against ₹545 crore of investment**, about 51% of investment recovered over roughly ten years | https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202511141127_SRJ_Strips_And_Pipes_Private_Limited.pdf | market view (rating agency), figures as reported by the agency |

## Why it matters
- The subsidy is being paid in practice on secondary steel in a backward zone. That makes it a useful data point for the "certainty of payment" axis when comparing Maharashtra with other states.
- The ratio (about half of investment returned over a decade) helps sense-check value bridges for D and D+ zone projects.
- Open: the yearly payment pattern and any delays are not in the rationale; check the earlier ICRA rationale and the state budget.
